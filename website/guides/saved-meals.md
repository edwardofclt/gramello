# Save and reuse your meals

Use **My meals** for a recipe or combination of foods you eat often. Save the
whole batch once, then choose your portion each time you log it.

## Build a meal

1. Open **Add food → My meals → Create meal** and enter a **Meal name**.
2. Tap **Add ingredient**, find a food, choose its amount and unit, and tap
   **Add ingredient** on the amount screen. Repeat for the whole recipe.
3. Check each ingredient amount. Under **Finished batch weight**, enter the
   cooked or prepared food's weight without its container, if you have it.
4. Under **Plan your portions**, choose **Equal servings**, **Grams per portion**,
   or **Ounces per portion**, then fill in the matching field.
5. Review the whole-batch and per-portion nutrition, then tap **Save meal**.

If every ingredient has a known weight, Gramello can estimate the batch weight.
Cooking can change that weight, so a measured finished weight gives a better basis
for portions. If an ingredient is measured by volume or has no known weight, a
finished batch weight is required.

Nutrition assumes the ingredients are evenly distributed throughout the meal.
For example, a batch weighing 800 g divided into four equal servings has a
200 g serving. You can still log a different amount later.

## Log a saved meal

1. In **Diary**, choose the date and tap the **+** next to the meal you want.
2. Open **My meals**. Use **Search my meals** to find your recipe, then select it.
3. Choose servings, grams, or ounces and enter the amount you ate.
4. Check the nutrition and selected diary meal, then tap its **Add to** button.

Creating a recipe saves it to your library. Logging it is a separate step.

## Edit or delete a recipe

In **My meals**, use the edit control beside a saved meal to change its name,
ingredients, batch weight, or portions, then tap **Save meal**. Use the delete
control and its confirmation to remove a recipe from the library.

Editing or deleting a saved recipe does not change food already logged in your
diary. To correct an old entry, [remove it and log the corrected portion](../logging-food/#correct-or-remove-an-entry).

## Keep your recipes when changing devices

Saved meals are included in a Gramello backup. Follow
[back up and move your data](../backups-and-data/) before moving to a new phone.
