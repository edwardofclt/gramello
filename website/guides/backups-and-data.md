# Back up and move your data

Your personal tracking data stays on your device. There is no Gramello account
or automatic diary sync between devices. Use a Gramello backup to keep a copy
or move your data to another phone.

## Export a complete backup

1. Open **Settings → Advanced** and find **Your data**.
2. Tap **Export backup**.
3. Use your device's sharing options to save the file somewhere you can access
   later, such as your chosen file storage.
4. Check that the file was saved before removing the app, clearing its data, or
   retiring the device.

The backup includes your diary, recipes, custom foods, water history, and goals.
It is a copy from the time you export it; later entries need a new backup.
Anyone with access to an exported file can read it, so choose its storage and
sharing destination carefully.

## Restore or move to another phone

1. Make the backup file accessible on the destination device and open Gramello there.
2. Go to **Settings → Advanced → Import backup** and select the Gramello backup file.
3. Review its date and record count in **Replace this diary?**.
4. If you want to replace the destination device's data, tap **Replace and import**.
   Choose **Cancel** to leave it as it is.
5. Open **Diary** and check your entries. Check **My meals** and **Settings** too.

**Import replaces rather than merges.** It replaces the diary, recipes, custom
foods, water history, and goals on this device. Export the destination device's
current data first if you want a separate copy. Installed food catalogs are unaffected.

## Recover the diary from before an import

Gramello keeps the current diary as a recovery copy when replacing it. If a
recovery copy is available, **Recover previous diary** appears in **Settings → Advanced**.

1. Tap **Recover previous diary**.
2. Read the replacement notice. Recovery also replaces the current personal data.
3. Tap **Recover diary** to proceed, or **Cancel** to keep the current diary.

Recovery is for the previous replacement; it is not a history of every edit or
an alternative to keeping exported backups.

## Export data for a spreadsheet

Choose **Export diary CSV** or **Export water CSV** in **Settings → Advanced**,
then save or share the file. CSV files are for reading in spreadsheets. Use
**Export backup** when you need a file that can restore the complete diary.

## Update the food catalog

Food information updates automatically when available. To check immediately,
open **Settings → Advanced → Food catalog → Check for updates**. Installed foods
remain available offline; downloading an update requires a connection.

Catalog updates do not sync or back up your personal diary. If you lose a device
without an accessible backup, Gramello support cannot restore its diary from a
server copy. See [support](../../support/) for help with an import or export error.
