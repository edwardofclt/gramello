# Log food your way

Start in **Diary**, choose the date, and tap the **+** next to the meal you want
to add to. Food is logged to that selected date, including when you are viewing
an earlier day.

## Search and choose a portion

1. In **Search foods**, enter at least two characters of a food, brand, or product
   name. Add a specific item name to narrow a long list of results.
2. Select a match. Check the brand, source, serving description, and nutrition.
   Use **View nutrition source** when it is available.
3. Choose Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snacks under **Add to meal**.
4. Choose an available unit and enter your amount. Review the updated calories,
   protein, carbs, and fat before saving.
5. Tap **Add to breakfast**, **Add to lunch**, **Add to dinner**, or **Add to snacks**.

Weight-based foods can offer grams and ounces; volume-based foods can offer
milliliters and US fluid ounces. Foods with no known weight may only offer
servings. An ounce by weight is different from a fluid ounce. Check the serving
description before assuming that one serving is a whole package.

A **Verified** label identifies a published source, not a guarantee that the
information matches your food. Check the package label and portion size.

## Scan or type a barcode

1. In **Add food**, tap **Scan barcode**.
2. Allow camera access if you want to scan. Hold the product barcode steady in
   the frame with good light.
3. To type instead, choose **Enter barcode manually**, enter the **Barcode number**,
   and tap **Look up barcode**. You can use the number field without camera access.
4. Review the returned food, select your portion and meal, and add it to the diary.

If there is no match, search by name or add a custom food. A successful scan reads
the barcode; it does not guarantee that the product exists in the food database.

## Add a custom food

1. In **Add food**, choose **Add custom food**.
2. Enter the food name, an optional brand, and a **Serving description**, such as
   “1 bowl” or “2 slices.”
3. Enter calories, protein, carbs, and fat for that entire serving. Add its weight
   in grams if you know it; otherwise leave the optional weight blank.
4. Tap **Save custom food**. Your food is saved on this device and labeled
   **Unverified**.
5. On the amount screen, choose how much you ate and tap the **Add to** button to
   log it. Saving the custom food alone does not add a diary entry.

## Correct or remove an entry

Open the right date in **Diary**, tap the trash icon beside the food, and confirm
with **Remove food**. To correct its portion or meal, remove the old entry and
add the food again with the right details. **Keep food** cancels removal.

## When search is unavailable

Downloaded foods, saved online results, and your custom foods remain available
offline. Online searches and new online barcode lookups need a connection.
If you see **Some nutrition databases are unavailable**, open **Show details**;
you can still select an available match or add a custom food.

For catalog updates, open **Settings → Advanced → Check for updates**. If a
specific food is incorrect, [contact support](../../support/) with its name,
brand, and source.
