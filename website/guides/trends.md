# Understand your trends

Open **Trends** to see the food you have logged over time. Charts and averages
use logged days only: a day without food entries is excluded, not counted as a
zero-calorie day.

## Choose a time range

1. Open **Trends** from the app navigation.
2. Choose **7 days**, **30 days**, or **6 months**.
3. Look at the logged-day count below the charts to see how much diary history
   contributes to that view.

If the charts are empty, log food in **Diary** for a date within the selected
range, then return to Trends. Water alone does not create a food-trend day.

## Read the summary numbers

- **Average calories** is the average of daily calorie totals for logged days
  in the range.
- **Goal-range days** is the percentage of logged days within 10% above or below
  your current calorie goal.
- **Average protein** is the average daily protein for logged days. The smaller
  comparison shows the difference from your current protein target.
- **Protein target** counts logged days with at least 90% of your current
  protein goal.

For example, if you logged food on three days in a seven-day range, the averages
use those three days. A day with only breakfast logged still counts as a logged
day, so incomplete entries can affect the picture.

## Read the charts

**Calories over time** compares logged calories with your current calorie goal.
**Daily macro mix** shows protein, carbs, and fat for logged days.

Changing your goals changes the comparisons, including for earlier dates.
Gramello does not infer which goal you had in the past. To update targets, follow
[water and goals](../water-and-goals/).

## Check an unexpected number

1. Go to **Diary** and use the date arrows to find the day in question.
2. Check for missing or duplicate foods and confirm the amounts and units.
3. Remove any incorrect entry and add the corrected food, then return to **Trends**.

Use trends as a view of your recorded food. They cannot tell whether an unlogged
meal was eaten or whether a day is complete.
