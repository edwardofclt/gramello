# Your first day with Gramello

You can use the Android and iOS apps without creating a Gramello account. Your
diary, saved meals, custom foods, water entries, and goals are stored on your device.

## Make it yours

1. Open Gramello and choose **Settings**.
2. Under **Daily goals**, enter your calorie, protein, carbs, and fat targets.
   Changing calories keeps your macro split; changing a macro recalculates calories.
3. Review all four values, then tap **Save daily goals**.
4. Under **Daily water goal**, choose your preferred water unit and enter a target.
   Tap **Save water goal** to save it separately.

The initial targets are defaults you can change. For more on how the fields work
together, see [water and goals](../water-and-goals/).

## Add your first food

1. Go to **Diary** and check the date at the top. Use the arrows to view an earlier
   day or return toward today.
2. Tap the **+** next to Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snacks.
3. Type a food or brand into **Search foods**, then choose a result.
4. Check the food details, choose an available unit, and enter the amount you ate.
5. Review the calories and macros, then tap **Add to breakfast**, **Add to lunch**,
   or the button for your selected meal.

Your food appears in that day's diary and updates its totals. For barcode scanning,
custom foods, and correcting an entry, see [logging food](../logging-food/).

## Find your everyday tools

- **Diary** shows food and water for the selected day. The **Water intake** card
  lets you log a preset amount or enter your own.
- **Trends** shows your nutrition over 7 days, 30 days, or 6 months.
- **Settings** holds your daily goals. **Settings → Advanced** has food catalog
  updates, backup export and import, and CSV exports.
- **Add food → My meals** holds recipes you can save and reuse.

## Keep a copy of your diary

Gramello does not automatically sync your diary between devices or keep a
server-side copy. Before changing phones or removing app data, use
**Settings → Advanced → Export backup** and save the file somewhere you can
access later. Follow [back up and move your data](../backups-and-data/) when
you are ready to restore it.
