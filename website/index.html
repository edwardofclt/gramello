Visit support ↗
Where can I use Gramello?

Gramello’s paid Android and iOS apps are coming soon. Each platform is a separate one-time purchase, and neither requires a Gramello account.

Is there a subscription or an extra paywall?

No. Android and iOS are each a one-time purchase, with all app features included. There are no subscriptions or additional feature paywalls. The two platforms are purchased separately; buying one does not include the other.

Where does the food information come from?

USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts, published restaurant nutrition, and custom foods you add. A Verified label identifies a published source; it is not a guarantee of accuracy. Always check the serving size and package label.

Do I need to scan my food?

Only if you want to. You can search by name, enter a barcode manually, or add a custom food. Camera access is optional, and barcode scanning happens on your device.

Can I use Gramello offline?

Your diary and personal tracking data are stored on your device. Online food searches and barcode lookups need an internet connection. Gramello does not provide account-based syncing between devices.

How do I manage or delete my data?

There’s no Gramello account to delete. Your diary and personal tracking data stay on your device; we don’t keep a server-side copy to access, export, or delete. Manage your entries in the app. For help or privacy questions, see your data and privacy.
